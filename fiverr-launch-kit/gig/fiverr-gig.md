## FAQ (paste into Fiverr FAQ section)

**Q: What do you need from me to start?**
A: One decent product photo (phone photo on a clean background works) or your product page URL, plus 1–2 sentences about your brand vibe. That's it.

**Q: Is this AI-generated? Will it look fake?**
A: I use a professional AI video pipeline, directed by hand — prompt design, shot selection, and edit passes are done by me. Look at the portfolio samples; that's the honest quality bar you'll get. If your product has fine text/logos, I'll show it in shots where it stays crisp.

**Q: Can you show my actual product, or is it a lookalike?**
A: Your actual product — I animate from your real photos. The closer your photo matches what you want on screen, the more accurate the result.

**Q: Do I get commercial rights?**
A: Yes, full commercial use for ads, socials, product pages, and marketplaces.

**Q: Can you make UGC-style videos with a person holding/using my product?**
A: Yes — the Standard and Premium tiers include UGC-style formats (unboxing, testimonial-style, try-on for apparel/footwear).

**Q: What products don't work well?**
A: Highly reflective jewelry with intricate stones and products where tiny label text is the hero can be hit-or-miss. Message me first and I'll tell you honestly before you order.
