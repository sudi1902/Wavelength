# Upwork Proposal Templates

Filter: posts <24h old, budget ≥$50, keywords "product video", "TikTok ad", "ecommerce video", "listing video". Skip anything asking for on-camera talent or 3D CAD animation.

## Template A — direct product-video job

> Hi [name],
>
> I make exactly this: cinematic product ad videos for e-commerce brands, delivered in 24–48h. Three samples of my current work: [links]
>
> For your [their product from the post], I'd open on [one specific visual idea pulled from their post — this line is 80% of why proposals win], then cut to [second beat], closing on your logo/CTA.
>
> I can deliver a 15s vertical (9:16) at 1080p with sound design for $[their budget or your price], including [1–2] revisions. If you'd like, I'll do the first concept frame before you commit.
>
> Happy to answer any questions — I respond within the hour.
> [Name]

## Template B — "need ad creatives for testing" (dropshippers/media buyers)

> Hi [name],
>
> Volume creative testing is my specialty — I run a pro AI video pipeline, so I can deliver 3–4 distinct creative angles per product (showcase / hyper-motion / UGC-style / unboxing) in 48h, instead of one video a week.
>
> Samples: [links]
>
> For [product], I'd test: (1) [angle], (2) [angle], (3) [angle]. Batch pricing: $[X] for 3 variations. If one angle wins, follow-up variations on that winner are $[Y] each.
>
> [Name]

## Template C — vague post, low detail

> Hi [name],
>
> Quick question before I pitch blind: is this for [guess A] or more like [guess B]? The right format differs a lot between the two.
>
> Either way, here's the quality bar I deliver for e-commerce video: [links]. 24–48h turnaround, 1080p, sound design included.
>
> [Name]

## Rules
- Never open with "I am a highly skilled professional…" — open with their product.
- One specific creative idea per proposal. That's the differentiator.
- Bid slightly UNDER their budget on the first 5 jobs (reviews > margin right now).
- Attach the most niche-relevant sample as the proposal attachment (beauty job → skincare sample).
